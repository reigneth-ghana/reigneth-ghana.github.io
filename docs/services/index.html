Our Services

Equipment banks and businesses rely on daily

We supply, install, and maintain shrink wrap machines, bank note banding machines, and coin counting machines — serving banks and businesses across Ghana.

Shrink Wrap Machines

We provide high-quality shrink wrap machines designed to protect your products efficiently and effectively. Our team is equipped to handle installations, repairs, and routine maintenance to ensure your operations run smoothly.

Ladypack Shrink Wrap Machine

A compact, angular packaging machine with a 90° heating oven, built to provide high-quality packaging with PVC, Polypropylene, Polyolefin, or Polyethylene film.

  • Impulse sealing system for centre-folded roll film
  • Nickel-chrome blade cuts and seals the film
  • Motorized conveyor belt feeds items into the shrink tunnel
  • Shrink tunnel heated by armored heaters

Adpak / Smipack SL Series

An entry-level L-sealing hood packer for clear-cut market segments, keeping most of the features of the S series at a lower output.

  • Two models available
  • Outputs of up to 300 packs/hour

Shrink wrap films

Packaging shrink films provide a tight, secure seal that helps prevent tampering or damage during transportation. We supply films made from PVC, Polyolefin, and Polyethylene in a range of thicknesses and sizes to match your product requirements.

Bank Note Banding Machines

Serving all banks across Ghana, we supply and maintain advanced bank note banding machines that ensure secure, efficient currency handling and help streamline cash management processes.

Akebono OB-360 Banding Machine

An arch-style tabletop machine for applying 28mm-wide paper bands around small bundled items, suited to low-volume requirements.

  • Up to 30 banding cycles per minute
  • Bands packages from 30mm × 10mm up to 420mm × 210mm
  • Works with either paper or film tape, no parts change needed
  • New track design supports 60-micron film, cutting cost and waste
  • Optional large dispenser available

Mabas SBM-90 Banding Machine

A heavy-duty automatic banknote banding machine from Mabas Electronics, with an integrated ink-jet band printer.

  • Prints bank name, branch name, operator name and date across 4 lines
  • 3 lines are operator-configurable (20 characters each)
  • Date/time line is system-generated and cannot be edited
  • Easy, quick, reliable and productive in daily use

Coin Counting Machines

Your one-stop-shop partner for banknote and coin processing — built for gaming, vending, banking, or any enterprise that receives bulk batches of identical coins or tokens.

Coin 100/120

A conveniently portable, cost-effective solution for single denomination processing. It processes large quantities of pre-sorted denominations swiftly and accurately.

  • Easy to set up and maintain
  • Simple double-dial setup for thickness and diameter
  • Continuous counting — the machine can be fed while running
