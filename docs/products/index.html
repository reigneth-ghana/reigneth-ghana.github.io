Shrink Wrap Machines

Compact, high-output packaging machines that protect your products efficiently and effectively.

Ladypack

A compact, angular packaging machine with a 90° heating oven, built to provide high-quality packaging with PVC, Polypropylene, Polyolefin, or Polyethylene film. An impulse sealing system with a nickel-chrome blade cuts and seals centre-folded roll film, and a motorized conveyor feeds items into a shrink tunnel heated by armored heaters. We supply several sizes and configurations to match your throughput:

Flagship

Ladypack 45N/A2

  • Our flagship shrink wrap machine — combined N/A2 configuration of the Ladypack 45 footprint
  • Touch-screen PLC control panel with guided cycle settings
  • Impulse-seal-and-tunnel design with foot-pedal operation and an integrated film-roll holder

Ladypack 45

  • Smallest footprint in the range — suited to lower-volume, bench-side packing runs
  • Two conveyor-belt cycles: forward-forward or forward-reverse, selectable from the panel
  • Handles polyethylene, PVC, polypropylene and technical films

Ladypack 65

  • Our most-supplied configuration — wider sealing table and taller package clearance than the 45
  • Same impulse-seal-and-tunnel design, built for steadier daily volumes
  • Electronic control with programmable tunnel temperature, belt speed and sealing time

Ladypack 110

  • The largest of the three — wider arch and higher throughput for high-volume floors
  • Suited to cash centres and bulk packaging runs where the 45 or 65 would bottleneck
  • Same conveyor-fed shrink tunnel design, scaled up

Ladypack Klok 550

  • Larger-format model in the Ladypack line, for bigger or bulkier items
  • Same impulse-seal-and-tunnel technology, scaled up for the 550 size

Ladypack Table Top

  • Compact bench/table-top configuration for lower-volume or space-constrained setups
  • Same impulse-seal-and-tunnel design as the rest of the range

Adpak / Smipack SL Series

The SL series is Smipack's entry-level L-sealing hood packer — designed for clear-cut market segments with a lower output, while keeping most of the features of the higher-output S series. We supply three models across the range:

Smipack SL45

  • Entry-level SL series — outputs of up to 300 packs/hour
  • Compact single-column hood packer, suited to lighter-duty use

Smipack S560N

  • Part of the S series — Smipack's core industrial range, for considerably higher throughput than the SL
  • Powered take-away conveyor for continuous, hands-off production

Smipack S560NA

  • Same S-series performance as the S560N, in an alternative chassis finish
  • Manual or automatic-cycle operation with a powered take-away conveyor

Shrink wrap films

Packaging shrink films provide a tight, secure seal that helps prevent tampering or damage during transportation. We supply films made from PVC, Polyolefin, and Polyethylene in a range of thicknesses and sizes to match your product requirements.

Bank Note Banding Machines

Advanced banding machines for secure, efficient currency handling and streamlined cash management, supplied to banks across Ghana.

Akebono OB Series

An arch-style tabletop machine for applying paper or film bands around small bundled items — banknotes, brochures, envelopes and similar flat items — using a heat-sealing system. We supply the OB-360, OB-460 and OB-560, three arch sizes built around the same reliable banding mechanism:

Akebono OB-360

  • Up to 30 banding cycles per minute
  • Bands packages from 30mm × 10mm up to 420mm × 210mm
  • Works with either paper or film tape, no parts change needed
  • New track design supports 60-micron film, cutting cost and waste

Akebono OB-460

  • Same heat-sealing mechanism as the OB-360, stepped up to a larger arch
  • Suited to bundles and printed-material runs that exceed the OB-360's capacity
  • Either paper or film tape, no parts change needed

Akebono OB-560

  • The largest arch in the range, for higher-volume banding floors
  • Same reliable heat-sealing mechanism as the OB-360 and OB-460
  • Optional large dispenser available

Mabas SBM-90

Mabas SBM-90 Banding Machine

A heavy-duty automatic banknote banding machine from Mabas Electronics, with an integrated ink-jet band printer.

  • Prints bank name, branch name, operator name and date across 4 lines
  • 3 lines are operator-configurable (20 characters each)
  • Date/time line is system-generated and cannot be edited
  • Easy, quick, reliable and productive in daily use

Alternative / Budget-Line Banding Machines

For customers who don't need the genuine Mabas SBM-90's integrated band printer, we also supply the DFM5540 and FM5540 — a separate, Chinese-manufactured line of automatic banknote banding machines at a lower price point.

DFM5540

  • Chinese-manufactured automatic banknote banding machine
  • Similar form factor and banding function to the genuine Mabas SBM-90, at a lower price point

FM5540

  • Chinese-manufactured automatic banknote banding machine
  • A step up from the DFM5540 within the same alternative line

Note: the DFM5540 and FM5540 are independently manufactured machines and are not Mabas products. Parts, firmware, and service procedures are not interchangeable with the genuine Mabas SBM-90 — let our technicians know which model you have when ordering spares or requesting service.
